licensed under MIT. The exact source revision and semantic asset mapping are
recorded in [third_party/fluentui-system-icons/NOTICE.md](third_party/fluentui-system-icons/NOTICE.md).

repository or any other third-party service.

`third_party/fluentui-system-icons/NOTICE.md`; it is not represented as a
Fluent UI System Icons asset and is not covered by the Fluent UI MIT notice.

redistribution.
